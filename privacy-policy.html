1. Introduction

BeelzeChat ("we", "us", "our") operates beelzechat.co.za — a South African social media and messaging platform. This Privacy & Content Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), South Africa.

By creating an account on BeelzeChat, you consent to the collection and processing of your personal information as described in this policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Provide the service — messaging, posts, groups, marketplace
  • Create and manage your account
  • Send push notifications for messages, likes, and comments
  • Process payments for subscriptions (R50/month) and banner ads (R1,000)
  • Improve our services and fix technical issues
  • Prevent fraud, spam, and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations (SARS — keep payment records for 5 years)

4. Where Data Is Stored & Protected

User Data & Messages — Firebase Firestore

Stored in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud), encrypted in transit (HTTPS/SSL) and at rest. Only authorized BeelzeChat administrators can access. Firebase is POPIA and GDPR compliant.

Images & Videos — Cloud Storage

Stored in encrypted cloud storage (Cloudinary / Supabase). Files are only accessible via secure URLs. Backed up automatically.

Payment Data — PayFast

All payments processed through PayFast (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant). BeelzeChat stores only the payment reference number — never credit card or banking details.

Push Notifications — OneSignal

Stores device notification IDs for sending push notifications. Does not store message content. Users can opt out anytime.

Security Measures

  • HTTPS/SSL encryption on all data transmission
  • Password hashing — passwords are never stored as plain text
  • Firebase Security Rules — restrict database access to authorized users
  • Access control — only the CEO/admin can access user data
  • Regular automated backups by Firebase

5. Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as necessary:

  • Active accounts: Data kept as long as the account is active
  • Deleted accounts: All data permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Messages: Kept until user deletes the conversation
  • Posts: Kept for 3 months (unless subscribed to permanent storage)
  • Payment records: Kept for 5 years (SARS legal requirement)
  • Analytics data: Kept for 13 months

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a BeelzeChat user, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data — view what we have about you
  • Correct inaccurate data — edit your profile anytime
  • Delete your account and all associated data — via Delete Account button
  • Object to certain data processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, email our Information Officer at ceo@beelzechat.co.za.

8. Data Breach Procedure

In the event of a data breach, we will:

  • Notify affected users within 72 hours
  • Report the breach to the Information Regulator of South Africa
  • Take immediate steps to secure the data
  • Provide guidance on how to protect yourself

9. Children's Privacy

BeelzeChat is intended for users aged 18 years or older. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately. Parents who believe their child has provided information can contact us at ceo@beelzechat.co.za.

10. Content Policy (Community Guidelines)

This section explains what content is allowed and not allowed on BeelzeChat.

✅ Allowed Content

You CAN post:

  • Business discussions and professional networking
  • Friendly conversations and social interaction
  • Photos and videos you own or have permission to share
  • Buying and selling legal items in the marketplace
  • Educational, entertainment, music, and lifestyle content
  • Breaking news (from reputable sources)
  • Respectful opinions and discussions

❌ Not Allowed Content

You CANNOT post:

  • Hate speech or discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or sexuality
  • Harassment, bullying, or threats of violence
  • Adult or pornographic content
  • Illegal goods (drugs, weapons, stolen items)
  • Spam, scams, or fraudulent content
  • Copyright infringement (posting others' content without permission)
  • Impersonation (pretending to be someone else)
  • Child exploitation — ANY content involving minors inappropriately
  • Malware, viruses, or harmful links
  • Content that promotes self-harm or suicide

Consequences of Violations

  • 1st offense: Warning + content removal
  • 2nd offense: Temporary suspension (7 days)
  • 3rd offense: Permanent account ban
  • Illegal content: Reported to the South African Police Service (SAPS)
  • Child exploitation: Reported to authorities immediately — zero tolerance

11. Reporting Content

If you see content that violates this policy, you can report it by:

  • Emailing support@beelzechat.co.za with a screenshot and details
  • Using the in-app report button on posts
  • Contacting our team via the app's profile section

Our team reviews all reports within 24-48 hours and takes appropriate action.
